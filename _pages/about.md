I'm a CS PhD student at Boston University studying the application of Geometry and Topology in ML and Graphics. I am advised by [Prof. Ed Chien](https://cs-people.bu.edu/edchien).

Outside of work, I enjoy bouldering, sailing, ice-skating, reading, biking, experimenting cooking new dishes, flying airplanes in Microsoft Flight Simulator.

### Research Interests

I am broadly interested in studying problems on the intersection of Stochastic Calculus, PDEs, Geometry and Machine Learning. My current research interest lies in building Monte-Carlo solvers for Non-Linear Hamilton Jacobi Equations for use in Reinforcement Learning applications.

You can find my CV [Here](../assets/pdf/Research_Resume.pdf). Please feel free to reach out with questions about my work, or to discuss interesting problems! 🙂 Email: [adrishd@bu.edu](mailto:adrishd@bu.edu).
